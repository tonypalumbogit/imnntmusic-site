IMNNT WEBSITE V19 — DIRECT VIDEO EMBED FIX

The Never Stop teaser is now embedded directly in /never-stop/index.html
as a native HTML5 <video> element.

Why:
The previous page depended on JavaScript replacing a placeholder, and a cached
older release.js could leave the page stuck on VIDEO LOADING.

V19 fixes this by:
- embedding the MP4 directly in the HTML
- keeping no-autoplay behavior
- showing native play controls
- using the Never Stop campaign artwork as the poster image
- renaming release.js to release-v19.js with cache busting
