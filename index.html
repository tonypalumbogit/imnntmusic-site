About

Heavy low-end.
Built for the peak.

For me, music has never been just about what you hear. It’s about what you feel. IMNNT is built around that idea — bass you feel in your chest, physical energy that moves through a room, and sound designed to hit on a real system.

As a partially deaf DJ, I experience music differently. The low end isn’t just something I hear — it’s something I feel. That perspective is part of the DNA behind IMNNT. Sets are built around tension, release, movement and physical impact — not just tracks in a playlist.
