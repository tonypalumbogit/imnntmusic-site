About

Heavy low-end.

IMNNT is a US-based DJ working across dubstep, hardcore / hard dance and tech house — genres designed to hit on a real system in a real room.

Sets are built as full arcs rather than playlists: tension, release, heavy transitions and deliberate changes in energy. Available for club nights, warehouse and rave-style events, festivals, support slots and private bookings.
